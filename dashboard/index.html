How the radar scores

Implied value follows only from the snapshot's own figures. For a protocol that records no token:

implied = TVL × 0.25 × growth × maturity × breadth × category

  • Growth — 1 + 0.5 × the seven-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+150%. Momentum, not hype.
  • Maturity — 0.55 + 0.45 × age in days (capped at 1,095 days). A protocol that has run three years with no token yet scores more than one listed last month. Past 1,460 days the factor is cut by 0.92: four years with no token yet is a sign the launch may never come. Unknown listing date counts as 365 days.
  • Breadth — 1 + 0.04 per chain the protocol runs on, up to six chains. Reach is users.
  • Category — 0.85–1.10 by token economics: fee-bearing categories (exchanges, lending, derivatives) support the largest launches; bridges and staking wrappers are pass-through products.

A protocol whose token already trades is never scored. It appears under Live tokens at the market's own value, so the board never mixes a traded price with a model's claim. DefiLlama's record is not proof: the board says a protocol records no token, never that it has none. Wrapped, staked and versioned products of tokens that already trade are left out of the ranked board entirely.

Farm plans are built from the row's own record — its category, its chains, its TVL and its age — never from a hand-written list of names. Capital follows TVL ($50–$500 a position), hold time follows age (30–60 days), gas follows the chain.

Data: DefiLlama public API, fetched once and sealed in the snapshot — the radar reads the same bytes every run. No network, no live prices, no prediction of whether or when a token is issued.
